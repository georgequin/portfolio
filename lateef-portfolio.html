Engineering decisions from data

I'm a Data Scientist and Risk Analyst with 5+ years of experience across fintech and insurance. I bridge the gap between raw data and business strategy — building predictive models, monitoring KPIs, and ensuring third-party vendors meet rigorous security and regulatory standards.

  • Cross-functional collaboration across engineering & compliance teams
